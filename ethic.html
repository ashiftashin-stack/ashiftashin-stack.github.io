Ethics in Information Technology

Ethics in IT focuses on responsible behavior when designing, developing, and using technology. Developers and data professionals must consider privacy, security, fairness, and transparency.

Key Ethical Principles

  • Privacy: Protect user data and avoid unauthorized access.
  • Security: Build systems that prevent breaches and cyber attacks.
  • Fairness: Avoid bias in algorithms and decision-making systems.
  • Transparency: Clearly explain how systems work and how data is used.

Guidelines for Developers

Developers should follow professional codes of ethics such as those outlined by major computing organizations. These include writing secure code, avoiding harm, and being accountable for the systems they build.
